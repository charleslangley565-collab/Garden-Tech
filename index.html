Connect probe to WiFi
Your probe creates a temporary WiFi network. Follow these steps on your phone.
1
Go to your phone's WiFi settings and connect to the network called GardenTech-Setup (password: gardentech)
2
Open a browser and go to 192.168.4.1
3
Enter your home WiFi name and password, then tap Save
4
The probe will restart and connect to your home WiFi. Reconnect your phone to your home WiFi network
🔍
Find your probe's IP address
You'll need the IP address to connect this app to your probe. There are two easy ways to find it.
A
Via Arduino IDE: Open the Serial Monitor (115200 baud) after the probe boots. Look for the line: Connected! IP: 192.168.x.x
B
Via your router: Log into your router's admin page and look for a device named GardenTech in the connected devices list
